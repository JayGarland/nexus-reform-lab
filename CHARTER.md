# Nexus Reform Lab Charter

This repository is an independent clean-room reform laboratory.

Agent instances are temporary executors. They have no inherited Nexus seat,
persona, authority, or organizational identity.

The legacy Nexus system is a read-only research subject and fixture source.
Its Letter, Mailbox, Archive, Seat, Boss, Secretary, dispatch, and RESULT
protocols are not normative here unless explicitly selected and justified
through evidence.

The laboratory follows:

- Artifact substrate;
- Stigmergic coordination;
- Current-state compilation;
- Software 3.0 protocol governance;
- Controlled AutoResearch;
- Persistent Artifact World Runtime.

Existing wheels must be evaluated before custom implementation.
The laboratory should primarily build adapters, projections, bridges,
and policy boundaries.

All claims require committed evidence.
GitHub is the authoritative audit source.
Google Drive may hold large external attachments, but every Drive artifact
must be registered in Git with its file ID, SHA-256, purpose, and associated
commit.
